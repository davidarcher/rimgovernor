## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: RG-HABITAT-003 — Develop facilities for the benefits they provide

The governor SHALL compare physical development with other useful colony work
through its expected consequences, including readiness, materials, shared labor,
operating costs and disruption. Benefits SHALL accrue only when usable provision
can produce them. Construction counts SHALL not substitute for useful outcomes,
and unread required facts SHALL prevent pricing the affected alternative.

#### Scenario: A facility cannot yet deliver its benefit

- **WHEN** a room or workstation needs unfinished construction, equipment, staffing or service before use
- **THEN** its comparison includes those prerequisites and their delay
- **AND** placing some of its components does not earn the completed facility's benefit

#### Scenario: Shared space and connectors support several functions

- **WHEN** functions could share a room or require corridors, doors or airlocks
- **THEN** the governor compares their actual effects, including access, travel, interference, disturbed sleep and thermal consequences
- **AND** shared prerequisites and benefits are accounted for without duplicate credit or spend
- **AND** a fixed room-compatibility table alone does not decide sharing

#### Scenario: No eligible user exists

- **WHEN** observed conditions establish that nobody can use a proposed facility during the assessed period
- **THEN** the facility receives no assumed benefit from those absent users
- **AND** known absence remains distinct from a missing required observation

#### Scenario: A service is down for a cause no work addresses

- **WHEN** a service is lost both to a cause no work addresses, such as a solar flare, and to damage that repair would fix
- **THEN** the repair is compared on its own merits, by when the service is next needed against the work's cost
- **AND** the cause nobody can fix neither defers the repair nor makes it urgent
