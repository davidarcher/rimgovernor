# Food Security Delta

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: RG-FOOD-008 — Feed people who need suitable food or help

The governor SHALL assess food suitability and access for each dependent,
including animals and people needing assistance. It SHALL arrange suitable food,
delivery and assisted feeding as needed, accounting for caregiver availability.
Available stock SHALL not establish feeding adequacy for a dependent unable to
reach, consume or receive it.

#### Scenario: A patient or baby cannot feed independently

- **WHEN** a dependent requires another pawn to provide suitable food
- **THEN** the governor accounts for a caregiver and the delivery and feeding work
- **AND** provisioning ingredients alone does not establish completed feeding

#### Scenario: Food exists outside the dependent's usable access

- **WHEN** a prisoner, penned animal or other dependent cannot access suitable available food
- **THEN** the governor arranges usable feeding access or delivery
- **AND** the inaccessible stock does not close that dependent's shortfall

#### Scenario: A baby's caregiver is occupied with competing work

- **WHEN** a baby needs feeding and a capable caregiver holds work whose continuation would delay feeding until starvation or its harmful effects
- **THEN** the governor weighs those consequences and arranges timely assisted feeding when a feasible response exists, including changing the caregiver's competing work when justified
- **AND** it accounts for travel, obtaining food and feeding time rather than waiting for an idle caregiver

#### Scenario: A breastfeeding-capable pawn cannot provide timely nursing

- **WHEN** a breastfeeding-capable pawn exists but milk, eligibility, access or caregiver time cannot cover a baby's feeding need in time
- **THEN** the governor considers other feasible nursing or suitable bottle-feeding, including needed provision
- **AND** the pawn's capability alone does not close the unmet need or suppress the assessment of alternative supply

#### Scenario: Babies compete for limited feeding capacity

- **WHEN** multiple babies need feeding from shared caregivers, milk or suitable food
- **THEN** the governor accounts for that finite capacity across their feeding work without promising the same capacity to incompatible uses
- **AND** any remaining shortfall stays unmet and contributes to reassessment

#### Scenario: Selected feeding is interrupted or its source becomes unavailable

- **WHEN** a feeding attempt is interrupted or its caregiver, milk or selected food becomes unavailable before adequate nutrition is received
- **THEN** the governor reassesses remaining demand using the baby's observed intake and condition and the current feasible alternatives
- **AND** selecting or starting a feeding action alone does not establish completed feeding

#### Scenario: No feasible caregiver or food source can meet the baby's need

- **WHEN** no available combination of a legal caregiver, usable feeding source and timely access can meet the baby's feeding need
- **THEN** the feeding need remains unmet and the governor exposes the material blocker through the shared explanation capability
- **AND** available colony stock alone does not establish feeding adequacy
