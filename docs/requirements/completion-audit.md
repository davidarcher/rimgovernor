[Collection](README.md) · [Review queue](review-queue.md)

This audit compares the collection with the original requirements-recovery
request and the subsequent direction to complete every mapped area. It assesses
the documentation task; it does not certify the product's gameplay.

This is the recovery-baseline audit. The later
[confirmed food policy](../../openspec/changes/redefine-food-requirements/proposal.md#decision-provenance-and-review-boundary)
replaces recovered food intent and expands the standing food requirements to
RG-FOOD-001–013. Food growing, hunting and assisted feeding are no longer inferred
intent candidates. The historical coverage and counts below do not certify those
expanded requirements; their implementation needs separate assessment.

## What the evidence means

The audit corrected two kinds of overstatement in the earlier collection.
Native save/load and cold-room cases existed where the draft had left inspection
unfinished. Conversely, several native tools existed beneath explicit planner
holds, so their operation tests could not establish autonomous support.

The resulting collection describes both. Representative implementation and test
inspection is complete across the map. Beyond the retained combat samples below,
current native acceptance results, sustained colony performance and visual
comprehension remain evidence investments with precise claims, not undiscovered
capability areas or claims of product failure.
